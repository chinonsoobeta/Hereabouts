How Hereabouts talks
Plain and warm. Like a housemate who remembers where everything is and never makes you feel bad for asking.

Words we use · words we don't

Items, assets, inventoryThings. "12 things in the garage."
Location, containerPlace. "It lives in the hall closet."
Deadline, expiration"Return by", "Warranty ends", "Goes off".
Resolve, archive, complete"Put back", "Returned", "Done".
Streak, broken streak, lost"Keeping up: 5 weeks." Never a loss, only a pick-up.
Upgrade, unlock, premium"Pay once." "Hereabouts Pro."
Error, invalid, failed"That didn't save. Try once more."
Enable, configure, settings"Turn on", "Change", "Settings" stays a noun.
Sync, cloud, account"Stays on your phone." "Backup."

Four rules

  1. Finish the user's sentence. The search field reads "Where is…" and they type the rest.
  2. Say the thing by name. "Rain jacket is due Wednesday", never "1 item is due".
  3. One sentence per screen state. If it needs two, the screen is wrong.
  4. Never praise, never scold. Report, then offer the next step.
