# Hereabouts: owner handoff

Everything that can be built and checked on this Mac is done or tracked in BUILD_STATUS.md. These items need the owner.

| Item | Why it needs you | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website and support email | The app links to `https://hereabouts.app` and `support@hereabouts.app`. These are placeholders, not registered. | Register a domain and mailbox, or pick existing ones. Set `HEREABOUTS_SITE_URL` and `HEREABOUTS_SUPPORT_EMAIL` in `project.yml`, then run `xcodegen generate`. |
| Privacy and support pages | App Store review requires a reachable privacy policy and support page. Drafts are in `Site/`. | Host `Site/privacy.html` and `Site/support.html` at `/privacy` and `/support` on the chosen site. Terms link to Apple's standard licence. Update the email address in both pages if it changes. |
| Apple Developer Program | The free Personal Team can't use TestFlight, App Store distribution or App Groups for the widget. | Enrol (US$99 a year), then set `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` in `project.yml`. |
| Due soon widget (B2) | A widget extension reads the notebook through an App Group, which a free Personal Team can't create. Siri, Spotlight and the Control Centre button are built and don't need one. | After enrolment, say so and the widget gets built. The plan is in FEATURE_PLAN.md B2. The app already opens `hereabouts://due` and `hereabouts://item/<id>`. |
| Shared notebook file (B1) | Built and tested: `NotebookFile.move` copies the notebook and its photos and checks the counts; the app reads changes other processes save and trims history only behind every reader. Wired in (`ac5d641`) but off: it runs once `HEREABOUTS_APP_GROUP` in `project.yml` names a group. Pro status goes to the group's settings then too. | After enrolment and settling the bundle ID: create the App Group (`group.` plus the bundle ID), add it to the app's entitlements and set `HEREABOUTS_APP_GROUP`. |
| Share sheet (B3) | The app side is built and tested (`fe79f08`): reading shared text, PDFs (first five pages) and photos, and the "which date, which thing" screen within the free limits. The extension that hosts it writes into the shared notebook, so it needs the App Group. | After enrolment and B1, ask for the extension target. |
| iCloud sync (B4) | The iCloud container needs the paid account's capability and a week on two devices. Built so far: the home's name lives in the notebook (B5), and Places offers to fold empty copies of a place into the original. | After enrolment, turn on the iCloud capability for the settled bundle ID, then ask for "Keep in iCloud". Then a week of use on your iPhone and a simulator on the same Apple Account. |
| Household sharing (B6) | Sharing a home uses CloudKit sharing, which needs B4. Built so far: homes (B5), and every move records who made it, shown once a home is shared. | After B4, ask for "Share this home…". Testing needs a second Apple Account; Angela, if she's willing. |
| Shelf photo names on your phone (A10) | "Several things in this photo?" only appears when Apple's on-device model is on and can read images. It's tested here with a stand-in; how good the names are can only be judged on a real phone. | On your iPhone 16 Pro Max with Apple Intelligence on: photograph a shelf in the new-thing screen, tap "Several things in this photo?" and say whether the names are worth keeping. |
| Reinstall before Sat 17 Oct, 12:11 PDT | Free-team installs stop opening after seven days. | Plug in the phone and ask for a reinstall. Builds need `HEREABOUTS_FREE_THINGS=200` and include the Control Centre extension. |
| App Store Connect products | Pricing changed on 12 Oct to a subscription (US$2.99 a month or US$19.99 a year, each with a two-week free trial) or US$49.99 for lifetime. The app and its local test store use the new plans; the real products don't exist yet. | Don't create the old US$4.99 product. Settle the bundle ID first, then create the three products listed under "Owner tasks" in `FEATURE_PLAN.md`. If the bundle ID changes, the product ID prefix changes in one place: `ProPlan.prefix` in `Localiser/Purchases/ProAccess.swift`. |
| App icon | The icon is still the pin-and-box mark from the Localiser days, one flat image with no dark or tinted version. It works, but it predates the notebook look, and iOS 26 draws dark, clear and tinted icons best from a layered Icon Composer file. | Decide whether the mark stays. Then make it in Icon Composer, or ask for a layered version of the current one. |
| Name clearance | "Hereabouts" needs a trademark and App Store name check. | Search the App Store and trademark registers before submitting. |
| TestFlight beta | Real people and real receipts can't be simulated. | After enrolment, upload a build and invite testers using `Docs/BETA_AND_RELEASE.md`. |
| Physical-device checks | Camera capture, real notifications over days, VoiceOver and older phones need hands on a device. | Follow the physical matrix in `Docs/BETA_AND_RELEASE.md` with synthetic documents first. |

Nothing has been uploaded, published, purchased or sent to anyone.
