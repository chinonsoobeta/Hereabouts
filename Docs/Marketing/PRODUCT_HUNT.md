## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I built Hereabouts because [the owner: one or two lines on why, in your own words].

It's a notebook, not a spreadsheet: save a thing and its place (Garage › Back wall › Rack 2), then search by name, a nickname or the place. A few things I'm proud of:

- **Lending.** Note who has it, pick a day to ask for it back, get a reminder, and text them in one tap. Phone numbers are never stored.
- **Trips.** Save a kit you pack often, check it off by place, then use the same list to put things away when you get home.
- **Private by design.** No account, ads or tracking. Your notebook stays on your device, with a backup or spreadsheet export anytime.
- iPhone and iPad, voice entry, a widget and Siri; in English, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese.

It's free for up to 15 things. Pro removes the limits (monthly, yearly with a free week, or Lifetime).

I'd love to hear what you'd keep track of first, and what's missing.
