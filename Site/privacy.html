Privacy policy

Hereabouts for iPhone. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Hereabouts doesn't collect any data. Everything you add stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Places, things, photos, notes and the history of where things have been.
  • Return, warranty and expiry dates, and the reminders scheduled for them.
  • Packing kits, trips and checklists.

Your iPhone's own backup to iCloud or a computer may include this data, under Apple's terms.

Camera, photos and documents

Hereabouts uses the camera or a photo you choose to picture a thing or read a date from a receipt. Text is read on the device. Unless you choose to keep the original, the image and text are discarded once you confirm a date.

Notifications and Calendar

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. By default they include the thing's name, which you can turn off in Settings. Hereabouts also sends a recap on Sunday evenings and a Still there? check once a month, which you can turn off in Settings, and a reminder the evening before a trip if something isn't packed yet. When you add a date to Calendar, Hereabouts asks for write-only access. It can add events but never reads your calendar.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

The names of your things and their places are added to your iPhone's Spotlight index, so you can find them by searching from the Home Screen. The index stays on your iPhone. When you ask Siri or run a shortcut, Apple handles your request under its own privacy terms, and Hereabouts answers from the library on your iPhone.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file or a spreadsheet. These files go only where you save or share them. They aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Purchases

Hereabouts Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and Hereabouts only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

Hereabouts doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions: support@hereabouts.app.
