Hereabouts support

Email support@hereabouts.app. You'll get a reply within two working days.

I'm not getting reminders

Open the Due tab and look at the Reminders card. If notifications are off, tap Open notification settings and allow them. iPhone queues a limited number of reminders at once, so open Hereabouts now and then to top up the queue, or add dates to Calendar.

How do I move my things to a new iPhone?

Restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings Hereabouts with it. You can also open Settings in Hereabouts, tap Take your notebook with you, and choose Back up everything. On the new phone, go to the same place and choose Restore from a backup.

What are the Sunday recap and the Still there? check?

On Sunday evenings Hereabouts sends one recap: what you added, what's due this week and what's drifting. On the first Saturday of the month it asks about a few things you haven't checked in a while. Turn either off in Settings, under Reminders.

I deleted something by mistake

Tap Undo in the message that appears right after deleting. After that, restore from a backup if you have one. To hide something without losing it, archive it instead.

Restoring Pro

Open Settings, tap Hereabouts Pro, then Restore purchases. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

What does the free plan include?

Up to 200 things, 2 packing kits and 5 receipt scans a month. Archived things don't count. Nothing you've saved is ever locked.
