Hereabouts

A home inventory app with no account

Every room, shelf and box, in a notebook you keep.

Places inside places

Add rooms, then the shelves, drawers and boxes inside them. Save a thing with its place, like Garage › Shelf 4, and find it later by its name, another name or the place.

More than one home

Keep a home, a cottage and a storage unit in one notebook. Move things, or whole boxes, between them.

Fast to fill in

Say where something is, like "the spare key's in the blue tin on the hall shelf", and check it before you save. Voice works in English for now. Or photograph a shelf and choose the names to add.

Private by design

No account, ads or tracking. Your notebook is saved on your device. Export a backup or a spreadsheet anytime. Mark a thing private to keep its place out of Spotlight and Siri's spoken answers.

iPhone and iPad

On iPad, places and things show side by side. Ask Siri "Where's my passport in Hereabouts?" on either.

Free for up to 15 things. Hereabouts Pro removes the limits. Pay monthly or yearly, with the first week free on yearly, or buy Lifetime once.
